## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, archival aesthetic tailored for an experienced software engineer and technologist. It rejects the stereotypical "dark terminal and neon cyber" tropes in favor of literary restraint, tactile warmth, and timeless print-inspired sophistication. The emotional tone is authoritative, thoughtful, crafted, and serene.

The style combines mid-century modernist editorial layout with modern interface precision:
- **Paper & Ink Foundation:** Off-white natural stock tones replace stark clinical whites, paired with rich charcoal inks instead of harsh pure blacks.
- **Literary Typography:** High-contrast display serifs signal narrative depth, intellect, and craft, balanced against utilitarian grotesques for flawless digital hierarchy.
- **Architectural Structure:** Structural layout is maintained via hairline rules and disciplined baseline grids rather than dropshadows or heavy card containers.
- **Target Audience:** Engineering leaders, design directors, founders, and discerning collaborators looking for craftsmanship, clear thinking, and architectural rigor.

## Colors

The palette mimics physical paper, binding boards, and carbon inks, illuminated by a disciplined terracotta accent used strictly for focal intention:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Warm White)
  - Secondary Container / Surface Raised: `#F4F1EA` (Warm Cream / Parchment)
  - Subtle Dividing Lines & Borders: `#E5E2DA` (Muted Warm Stone)
  - Deep Surface / Inverted Surface: `#18181B` (Charcoal Carbon)

- **Typography & Content:**
  - Primary Ink: `#18181B` (High-contrast charcoal, avoids digital harshness)
  - Secondary Ink: `#52525B` (Muted slate neutral for supporting paragraphs)
  - Tertiary / Caption Ink: `#71717A` (Quiet warm gray for metadata and timestamps)

- **Accents:**
  - Key Accent: `#C2410C` (Burnt Terracotta) for primary interactive highlights, focus states, and key tags.
  - Supporting Accent: `#D97706` (Ochre Amber) for warning tags, commit metadata, and hover nuances.
  - Hover / Active Accent: `#9A3412` (Deep Terracotta) for tactile hover states.

## Typography

The typographical pairing negotiates between classical publishing and utilitarian engineering:

- **Playfair Display:** Employed exclusively for case-study titles, mastheads, section titles, and thematic quotes. It brings bookish authority and human warmth. Display sizes must preserve negative tracking to enhance the print-like density.
- **Inter:** The structural workhorse for narrative prose, UI components, navigation, and body copy. Selected for its neutral legibility and rigorous metrics.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Reserved strictly for technical metadata, commit references, technology stacks, metrics, and inline terminal snippets. It must never be used for extended reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is informed by asymmetric editorial grids and generous structural margins:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (< 768px): 4-column layout with 1.25rem (`margin`) gutters.
  - Tablet (768px – 1024px): 8-column layout with 1.5rem (`gutter`) gutters and 2.5rem margins.
  - Desktop (> 1024px): 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of 1280px, with 2.5rem (`gutter-desktop`) gutters and 5rem (`margin-desktop`) margins.
- **Rhythm & Rules:**
  - Sections are partitioned using 1px horizontal stone rules (`#E5E2DA`) rather than arbitrary boxed cards.
  - Vertical rhythm leverages `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` between content sections to allow reading cadence to breathe.
  - Editorial asymmetry: Case study layouts place index columns (client, role, year, tech) in a sticky 3-column side rail, while project narratives occupy the remaining 9 columns.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids skeuomorphic dropshadows, blurred glass backdrops, or floating neon elevations. Depth is achieved via physical print metaphors:

- **Surface Tonal Stacking:**
  - Base level: `#FAF9F6`.
  - Inset panels / Code previews / Secondary cards: `#F4F1EA` flat surface fill.
  - Hover / Interactive highlight: Barely perceptible warmth transition from `#FAF9F6` to `#F4F1EA`.
- **Delicate Hairlines:** Boundaries are defined strictly by 1px solid `#E5E2DA` rules.
- **Transient Depth:** When elements demand active elevation (such as command menus, modals, or image lightboxes), use an unblurred structural shadow tinted with warm charcoal:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03);`
  - Modals feature a solid or low-opacity wash: `background: rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.88)` with backdrop filter kept subtle (blur <= 4px) to avoid synthetic glass effects.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a restrained **Soft (`1`)** shape language:

- **Corners:** Default controls, inputs, and container elements feature a tight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. Structural cards and modal dialogs use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **No Pill Shapes:** Pill-shaped or fully circular geometries are banned for interactive buttons or containers; they disrupt the typographic, bookish atmosphere.
- **Monastic Precision:** Tag badges and technical indicators should use clean right angles (0px) or the soft 4px boundary, mirroring library reference labels.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action:** Solid charcoal `#18181B` surface with `#FAF9F6` text, 4px corner radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, Inter 14px weight 500. On hover, background shifts to `#C2410C` (burnt terracotta).
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined with 1px `#E5E2DA`, background transparent or `#FAF9F6`, text `#18181B`. On hover, subtle surface wash of `#F4F1EA` with border darkening to `#18181B`.
- **Editorial Text Links:** Natural charcoal text with an underline offset of 4px using a 1px solid terracotta (`#C2410C`) underline. Smooth transition to bold terracotta on hover.

### Chips & Technical Tags
- **Code & Stack Chips:** JetBrains Mono 12px (`code-sm`), uppercase or lowercase with technical precision. Background `#F4F1EA`, border 1px solid `#E5E2DA`, text `#52525B`.
- **Featured / Status Tags:** Background `#FAF9F6`, border 1px solid `#C2410C`, text `#C2410C` with a small leading 6px terracotta dot.

### Lists & Project Rows
- **Archive Project Lists:** Horizontal list items separated by 1px top rules (`#E5E2DA`). Columns align title (Playfair Display), role/category (Inter body-sm), stack (JetBrains Mono code-sm), and year.
- **Hover State:** Project row subtly transitions background to `#F4F1EA` with an arrow glyph (`→`) translating 4px to the right.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Fields:** Background `#FAF9F6`, border 1px solid `#E5E2DA`, text `#18181B`, placeholder text `#71717A`. Radius 4px. Focus state triggers border color `#C2410C` with an outline of 2px offset terracotta glow at 15% opacity.
- **Command Palette:** High-density, minimal modal box with 1px border `#E5E2DA`, `#FAF9F6` background, featuring monospace keyboard shortcuts (`⌘K`).

### Cards & Case Previews
- **Case Study Cards:** Free of drop shadows. Framed by a 1px solid `#E5E2DA` border and `#F4F1EA` image background container. Inner card padding is generous (`space-xl`).
- **Typography Stack inside Cards:** JetBrains Mono date/index stamp at top, Playfair Display title, Inter secondary body description, followed by monospace tag cluster.
